Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Kozlowski Financial Coaching LLC ("Kozlowski Financial", "I") runs this website. This page explains what information the site collects and what I do with it.

What I collect

When you request a consultation, the form collects your name, email, phone number (optional), preferred date, the service you're interested in, your organization (optional), and what you write about your situation.

Please don't include account numbers, Social Security numbers, passwords, or other sensitive identifiers in the form. We'll cover the details you're comfortable sharing during your call.

The site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies of its own.

How it's used

I use your information only to respond to your request, schedule a session, and provide coaching services you sign up for. I don't sell, rent, or share your information for marketing.

Where it's stored

The site is hosted by Netlify. Form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify and forwarded to my email, hosted by Google. Both services encrypt data in transit and at rest, and access to these accounts is protected with multi-factor authentication. Page styling is loaded from Tailwind's content delivery network, which receives standard request information such as your IP address.

How long it's kept

Form submissions are deleted from the website host once they've been moved into my client records. If you don't become a client, I delete your information on request, or after 12 months.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information I have about you by emailing KozlowskiFinancial@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above.
